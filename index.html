About

Advancing precision medicine through reliable, interpretable, and clinically actionable statistical learning methods.

I am a PhD candidate in Biostatistics & Bioinformatics at Emory University and a former medical AI research scientist with 3+ years of industry R&D. My work combines statistics with scalable machine learning to analyze high-dimensional biomedical data across genomics, single-cell biology, medical imaging, and clinical applications.

My research focuses on developing methods that turn biological and medical data into interpretable insights. Before my PhD, I built production deep-learning pipelines for brain MRI, authored the clinical validation reports behind an FDA 510(k) clearance, and co-invented three granted U.S. patents.

Currently seeking Statistician / ML Scientist / Research Scientist roles at the intersection of biomedical AI, statistical learning, and precision medicine.
